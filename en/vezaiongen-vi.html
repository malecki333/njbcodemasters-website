INDUSTRIAL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM

VezaionGen VI

A modular platform for connected industrial workflows.

VezaionGen VI brings capabilities for production, quality, maintenance, logistics and energy into a shared digital working context.

Core Capabilities

Industrial Core

Production, materials, orders and quality information in one operational context.

Digital Twin & Robotics

Digital representations of assets and factory areas, alongside capabilities for robotic workflows.

Industrial Analytics

Operational dashboards for data, anomalies and insights as a basis for analysis.

Autonomous Operations

Event-driven workflows, simulation and recovery with human hand-offs built into the operational approach.

Knowledge Systems

Structure industrial rules, data contracts and operational knowledge for teams.

Logistics, Quality & Energy

Bring logistics workflows, quality information, and energy and emissions data into the platform context.

Explore modules

Architecture

Shared data models and API contracts connect modular applications, operational information and external systems. Role-based interfaces help teams work with information across functions and locations.

Deployment

Product planning includes On-Premise, Private Cloud and SaaS models. Infrastructure, integrations and operating workflows are discussed in the context of each intended use.

Pilot Program

A pilot starts with a specific operational use case. Goals, participants, relevant data and a shared evaluation framework are aligned before the project begins.
