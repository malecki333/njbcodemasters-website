Website delivery, hosting (GitHub Pages) and domain (IONOS)

This website is publicly accessible internationally. Pages and files are delivered via GitHub Pages, while domain management is handled through IONOS. When you access the website, technically required connection and access data (such as IP address, timestamp of the request, requested URL or file, browser/device information and referrer) may be processed by the underlying infrastructure to ensure delivery, stability and operational security. Because GitHub Pages operates an international / US-based infrastructure, processing or transfer of technical connection data to third countries (including the United States) cannot be ruled out. Currently, this website does not embed web analytics or advertising tracking scripts.

Legal bases and international context

Where personal data is processed in connection with using this website, such processing is carried out – depending on applicable law (in particular the Serbian Law on Personal Data Protection, Zakon o zaštiti podataka o ličnosti, and, where individuals in the EU/EEA are concerned, the EU General Data Protection Regulation [GDPR]) – based on the legitimate interest in providing the website securely and reliably or to handle direct enquiries.

Contact enquiries

The contact form on this website opens an email draft in the visitor’s email application. The form does not transmit the entered details to a website mail server. The visitor reviews and sends the draft. If you write directly to nenad@njbcodemasters.com, the information you provide is used to process your request.

Local browser storage and cookies

This website stores your preferences for language, display (theme), sound and notice status locally in your browser (for example via local browser storage). No analytics, tracking or marketing cookies are set at present. You can clear locally stored entries at any time in your browser settings.

Newsletter and AI Assistant

The newsletter form does not currently accept subscriptions. The AI Assistant is not currently connected to an external AI service and does not transmit user input to external AI servers.

External links

This website contains links to external services and platforms (such as LinkedIn or email links). When you follow external links, the privacy policies and terms of the respective third-party provider apply.

Data subject rights and supervisory authority

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to request access to processed personal data concerning you, rectification of inaccurate data, erasure or restriction of processing, objection to certain processing activities and – where applicable – data portability. Please send such requests to nenad@njbcodemasters.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority (in the Republic of Serbia, in particular the Commissioner for Information of Public Importance and Personal Data Protection / Poverenik za informacije od javnog značaja i zaštitu podataka o ličnosti, or the competent authority in your place of residence).
