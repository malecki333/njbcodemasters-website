What is VezaionGen VI?

VezaionGen VI is NJB CodeMasters’ modular industrial platform built for measurable operational impact. It brings factory operations, quality, maintenance, energy, robotics, and logistics together in one unified context—so teams respond faster, eliminate data silos, and make reliable decisions.
