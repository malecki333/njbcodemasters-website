What is VezaionGen VI?

VezaionGen VI is an integrated industrial web platform by NJB CodeMasters. It connects documented functions for factory operations, quality, maintenance, energy, robotics and logistics through shared data models and user interfaces.

Modules · Pilot Program · Roadmap

Why Industry 6.0?

Production, transport, quality and energy connect in a shared operational context. Common data models, orchestrated workflows and clear recovery paths help teams understand change and coordinate responses.

Industry, Connected

From shared data models to modular deployment, VezaionGen VI brings industrial information flows into a coherent working context. Explore the product page for its capabilities, modules and deployment options.
