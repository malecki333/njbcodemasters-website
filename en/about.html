NJB CodeMasters

Software development for connected industry and digital products.

Mission

We connect software, data and operational workflows so industrial teams can work with information and tools in a clear digital context. With VezaionGen VI, we are developing our platform for connected industrial operations.

Vision

We envision an industry where people, machines and intelligent systems work together productively. Industry 6.0 represents adaptable, connected and human-centred industrial solutions.

Founder

Nenad Baralić founded NJB CodeMasters and leads the company. His work brings together business direction, software development and the goal of building digital products for industrial applications.

Research

Our research interests include industrial data models, Digital Twins, operational analytics, Industrial AI and collaboration across connected systems. We explore how these approaches can make operational information clearer and more useful.

Development

We develop software modules, interfaces and workflows for web, enterprise and industrial applications. VezaionGen VI brings industrial capability areas together in a shared platform.

Innovation

For us, innovation grows from combining industrial practice, thoughtful software development and emerging technology. We see Digital Twin, Industrial AI and automation as building blocks for useful digital products.

Future Objectives

We aim to develop VezaionGen VI with industry partners, shape relevant pilot projects and expand the platform step by step around connected industrial workflows.
