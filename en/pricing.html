Frequently Asked Questions

Is maintenance included?

The listed prices cover website creation. Maintenance and support are optional and can be added as a monthly package.

Can I change the services?

Yes. The packages are guidelines. We can adapt the scope and cost to your specific requirements.

How long does a project take?

Standard projects take 6–8 weeks. Express (4–6 weeks) and urgent (2–4 weeks) options are available at an additional charge.

Are there hidden costs?

No. All costs are transparent. Following a detailed assessment, you receive a binding quotation without surprises.
