VEZAIONGEN VI

Modules for industrial work

Each module resolves concrete operational bottlenecks, provides immediate visibility and connects cleanly with your existing processes.

Industrial Core

What it does: Unifies production, materials, orders, maintenance and quality data in one place.

For whom: Production, maintenance and plant leadership teams.

Why: Shorter lead times, clearer priorities and less friction across manufacturing, maintenance and quality.

Digital Twin & Robotics

What it does: Models equipment, factory zones and robotic tasks digitally and spatially.

For whom: Engineering, automation and facility planning teams.

Why: Spatial dependencies and automated workflows become verifiable upfront, safer and easier to plan.

Logistics & Transport

What it does: Consolidates transport, routing, fleet and dispatch information end to end.

For whom: Logistics, transport and supply-chain teams.

Why: Dispatch, loading and execution align seamlessly—eliminating information gaps between planning and the road.

ESG & Sustainability

What it does: Captures and maps energy and emissions data directly to operational areas.

For whom: Energy, sustainability and operations leaders.

Why: Consumption and metrics become transparently manageable and ready for reporting and compliance.

Autonomy & Recovery

What it does: Supports event-driven workflows, simulation and guided recovery.

For whom: Operations, process and automation teams.

Why: Disruptions and deviations are caught faster while teams retain full control and clear hand-offs.

Integration Ecosystem

What it does: Connects applications via clean APIs, data adapters and synchronization.

For whom: IT, OT, engineering and integration teams.

Why: Existing systems and sites work together without interrupting ongoing operations.
