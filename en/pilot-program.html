Pilot phases

A structured cadence keeps every step predictable, sets clear expectations and produces verifiable results:

1. Use case

Define the workflow, bottlenecks or data gaps and intended outcomes together.

Outcome: A focused pilot scope with measurable goals.

2. Planning

Align on participants, relevant data, interfaces and the concrete working boundary.

Outcome: A reliable project and evaluation plan.

3. Exploration

Evaluate selected capabilities and workflows within the agreed project scope.

Outcome: Documented findings on workflow fit, usability and impact.

4. Review

Assess results, open questions and options for scaling or rollout together.

Outcome: A sound decision basis for your next steps.
