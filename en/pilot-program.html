Pilot phases

1. Use case

Describe the workflow, starting context and intended objectives together.

Outcome: An agreed pilot focus.

2. Planning

Define participants, data, integrations and project scope.

Outcome: A shared project and evaluation framework.

3. Exploration

Consider selected capabilities in the agreed project context.

Outcome: Documented observations about workflow and usability.

4. Review

Discuss findings, open questions and options for continuation.

Outcome: A shared basis for deciding possible next steps.

Working together

The operations team contributes process knowledge and day-to-day context. NJB CodeMasters helps explore platform capabilities, information flows and integrations. Goals and evaluation criteria are defined together.

Results

The pilot creates a shared view of the selected use case, the capabilities considered and the resulting observations. Scope, schedule and commercial terms are agreed for each initiative.
